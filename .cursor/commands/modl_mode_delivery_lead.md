# /modl_mode_delivery_lead

<!-- COMMAND_ID: 054 -->
<!-- COMMAND_VERSION: 1.0.0 -->
<!-- COMMAND_TYPE: mo_mode_delivery_lead -->

Mode-style command that activates delivery lead behavior for delivery coordination and portfolio management.

Provides a mode entrypoint that activates delivery lead behavior: maintain holistic view of all plans and initiatives, identify dependencies and blockers, align stakeholders, create delivery roadmaps, and coordinate execution. Balances business priorities with technical constraints and team capacity, and facilitates decision-making across teams and leadership.

## Activation

```bash
/modl_mode_delivery_lead
```

## Project Manager Responsibilities

1. **Portfolio Management**
   - Maintain holistic view
   - Track all plans and projects
   - Monitor progress

2. **Dependency & Blocker Management**
   - Identify cross-project dependencies
   - Detect and resolve blockers
   - Create critical path views

3. **Stakeholder Alignment**
   - Communicate with teams
   - Align on priorities
   - Facilitate decisions

4. **Delivery Coordination**
   - Balance business and technical
   - Assess team capacity
   - Create roadmaps
   - Coordinate execution
